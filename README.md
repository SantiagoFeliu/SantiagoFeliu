## Why operations-first engineering matters

Most automation tools prioritize feature velocity. I prioritize **reliability under uncertainty**:

- **Sustain** reliable operations. Systems must be observable, debuggable, and recoverable.
- **Extend** capabilities through purposeful automation. Every change is reversible; every outcome is verifiable.
- **Deliver** controlled, traceable results. Diagnose before modifying. Verify before declaring success.

Twenty years coordinating aviation, health, and emergency operations taught me that a system's true measure is not "does it run," but "can I understand why it failed, and can I fix it safely?"
